## Decision

**Primary: Shieldex (Statex, Bremen).**
1. German production → matches Berlin brand story, EU supply chain, no China MOQ risk.
2. Silver-plated polyamide is skin-safe → covers Wear + Baby Canopy without legal headaches.
3. 60+ dB = kills 5G/LTE/GPS/wifi/BT/NFC with margin (phone drops to zero bars well before -60 dB).
4. They already build shielded tents and pouches themselves → possible OEM/co-manufacturing partner for the Tent (€1,900 price point sits in the empty gap: consumer popup tents at $660–1,400 (YSHIELD SAFECAVE) vs forensic tents at $19k–47k (Faraday Defense)).

**Secondary (margin play):** NiCu ripstop at $3.50–6/m for Tent/Curtain/Blanket/Canopy linings once volumes justify the 200 m MOQ. Cost per tent lining ≈ €15–25 fabric vs Shieldex ≈ €300+.
